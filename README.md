# My Favorite Movie: Forrest Gump

**Forrest Gump** is a 1994 American comedy-drama film directed by Robert Zemeckis and starring Tom Hanks as the title character.

## Main Characters
* **Forrest Gump** - A kind-hearted and slow-witted Alabama man who accidentally influences several historical events.
* **Jenny Curran** - Forrest's lifelong love interest and childhood best friend.
* **Lieutenant Dan Taylor** - Forrest's commanding officer in Vietnam who loses his legs.

## Memorable Quotes
> "Mama always said life was like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're gonna get." — Forrest Gump

## Iconic Moments
1. Running across the United States for over three years.
2. Playing ping-pong for the American team in China.
3. Receiving the Medal of Honor in Vietnam.
